% Controls: the table of actions (keyboard / joystick), then notes.
Use the cursor keys and the keys below, or a joystick in port~1. They all work at the same time.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{\tAction} & \textbf{\tKeyboard} & \textbf{\tJoystick}\\
\midrule
Walk (8 directions) & \key{$\uparrow$} \key{$\downarrow$} \key{$\leftarrow$} \key{$\rightarrow$} & stick\\
Fire the rifle & \key{SPACE} or \key{Z} & button 1\\
Throw a grenade & \key{M} or \key{X} & button 2\\
Start a normal game (title) & \key{1}, or the fire keys & button 1\\
Start an easy game (title) & \key{2}, or the grenade keys & button 2\\
Skip the cover & \key{SPACE} & button 1\\
Pause / resume (in a game) & \key{P} & ---\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Rifle.} It never runs out. Each press fires a short burst of two shots in the direction you are facing; press
again and again for more. Holding the button down does not keep firing.
\item \textbf{Grenades.} They always fly towards the top of the screen, whichever way you face, land about half a screen
ahead and explode there. They fly over sandbags and walls. You can throw a new one only when the last one has exploded.
\item \textbf{Walking up} moves the map: the game only scrolls forwards, never back.
\item \textbf{Pause.} \key{P} stops the game and its sound; press \key{P} again to carry on.
\end{itemize}
